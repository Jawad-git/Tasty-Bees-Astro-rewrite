## Our story

TastyBees began as a juice bar with a sweet tooth. Over the years the menu grew to include
crêpes, pancakes and waffles, scoops and sundaes, and the celebration cakes our guests now
order for birthdays and family gatherings.

## What we care about

- **Fresh fruit, every day.** Juices and cocktails are prepared to order, never from concentrate.
- **Generous portions.** Our crêpes and pancakes are made to be shared, though you won't want to.
- **Coffee done properly.** From a classic espresso to our signature pistachio iced coffee.
- **Cakes for every celebration.** Whole cakes in medium and large, and trays of mini sweets for your soirée.
