## A little sweeter, every day

TastyBees started with a simple idea: generous desserts, honest fruit and coffee made with care.
Our juices are squeezed to order, our crêpes and pancakes are filled to the edge, and our
celebration cakes are finished by hand with fresh fruit.

Drop by for a **breakfast bundle** before 2 PM, cool down with a Spanish latte, or order a
whole cake for the people you love.
